How It Works

  1. Draw dark lines on the template above (floors, walls, platforms)
  2. Open Sketchbook AR on your phone
  3. Point the camera at your drawing
  4. Tap "Capture Paper" to scan your level
  5. Adjust the threshold slider until only your lines are visible
  6. Tap left/right sides of screen to walk, center to jump

Drawing Tips

  • Draw continuous horizontal lines for stable floors
  • Leave gaps for jumps — but not too wide!
  • Walls should be tall and thick (vertical lines)
  • Avoid thin, light pencil — it may not register
  • Keep drawings within the dashed box above
